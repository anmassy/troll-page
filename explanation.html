😊 What Just Happened

🎯 You experienced a simulation!

The "scary" page you saw was an educational demonstration to show you how cybercriminals might try to frighten you.

What the page did:

  • 🌐
    Retrieved your IP address
    Normal - all websites see your IP (like your postal address on the internet)
  • 💻
    Detected your browser
    Public information - like knowing if you use Chrome or Firefox
  • 📱
    Saw your screen resolution
    To adapt the display - all responsive sites do this

🛡️ Rest assured:

No personal data was stolen! No virus installed! It was just a fun way to raise awareness about cybersecurity.

🎓 Why This Demonstration?

The idea was to show you in a playful way how easy it is to scare people on the internet with information that is actually... normal!

Real dangers to avoid:

  • 🎣
    Phishing
    Fake emails that look like your bank
  • 💳
    Banking information theft
    Sites that ask for your card codes
  • 🔐
    Password theft
    Malicious applications that record what you type

💡 The Simple Lesson

🤔 Before clicking on a weird link, ask yourself:

  • Do I really know this person?
  • Why are they asking me to click there?
  • Does this smell like a scam?

Super Simple Tips:

  • 📞
    When in doubt, call!
    If "your friend" asks for money via message, call them
  • 🔗
    Check the links
    If it says "facebook.com" but the URL looks weird, be suspicious
  • ⏰
    Take your time
    Real services never say "Click NOW or else..."
